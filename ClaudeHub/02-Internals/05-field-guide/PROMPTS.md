# Prompts that work

Copy, paste, change the parts in `<brackets>`. Open the folder named in brackets first, when
one is given. Add your own when one works well for you. Claude drafts and prepares; you post,
send, and say ok to Hub updates. None of these make it do any of that for you.

## The Hub, daily  `[01-AIDesktop\02-Work\hub]`
- `What's waiting on me?`
- `Open <teammate>'s handoff and tell me what they want and what you'd do.`
- `Go.` (after Claude says what it would do)
- `Hand this back to <teammate> with a note: done / next / what's left.`
- `Hand <file or folder> to <teammate> on our shared space, with a note.`
- `Draft my update for today: shipped / next / blocked.` then `Post it.`
- `Check if <teammate> or their agent updated anything and tell me what needs an answer.`
- `My Hub key expires soon. Walk me through making a new one.`

## Your work  `[your desk in 02-Work]`
- `Build my first desk.` (first visit, in `02-Work`)
- `Here's today's <batch / list / notes>: <paste or point at the folder>. Run it through the desk.`
- `What's stuck? Anything with no next step or gone quiet?`
- `Draft <a caption / a message / a reply> in my voice. Give me three versions, each testing a different idea.`
- `That's not how it works: <how it really works>. Fix the desk's files and anything built on it.`
- `Weekly review: what did I do, what worked, what should I stop?` (honest numbers, say how many items each claim rests on)

## Selling for TerraByte  `[your desk]`
- `Research <company> before my call. Label what is public fact and what is your guess.`
- `Draft a first message for <lead>, in my voice. Use [UNKNOWN - ask Tate] anywhere Tate hasn't given us the answer in writing.`
- `Here are my call notes: <paste>. Turn them into a one-page brief for Tate, unknowns first, stated vs inferred.` then `Hand it to Tate in the Hub.`

## Research  `[any folder]`
- `/research <question>` for a quick, sourced answer.
- `/deep-research <question>` for the heavy version (uses a lot of your plan).
- `Find out whether <platform rule / tool / claim> is what people say it is, with sources.`

## Side projects  `[a new folder]`
- `I want to build <thing>. Interview me, then write a one-page brief in a new project folder.`
- `Cut the brief into milestones I can each see working in one session.`
- `Next milestone. Plan it first and show me the plan in plain words.`
- `Open it and show me it working. What did you test, and what didn't you?`
- `Commit and push.`
- `Set up a room for <side project> in my 01-Personal folder.` (uses the side-project starter)

## Games  `[01-Personal or any folder]`
- `Research the current patch / meta for <game>. Cite the patch date.`
- `Help me build a tracker / overlay / spreadsheet for <game thing>.` (check the game's rules on third-party tools first; ask Claude to)

## Fixing
- `Stop. Find out why this failed before you change anything.`
- `Undo what you just did.` (or Esc twice to rewind)
- `This used to work. What changed?`
- `The Hub stopped working.` (in `02-Internals\07-hub`)

## Reviewing  `[a fresh session]`
- `Review <project> as a skeptic: what's broken, missing, or overcomplicated?`
- `Check every number on this screen against the files.`
- `Read my last five drafts for anything claimed about TerraByte that Tate didn't give us in writing.`

## Free tools  `[04-Toolshed]`
- `Is there a free tool on GitHub that <does X>? Check it's safe before installing anything.`

## Learning  `[any folder]`
- `Explain what you just did in five lines.`
- `What could you do for me that I haven't asked for yet?`
- `What does <word> mean?`

# Experiment ideas

Delete or mark each one when it is done. Each is a single-file experiment; ask for it by name.
Labels: **[work]**, **[hub]**, **[build]**, **[fun]**. Fun ones are for fun and promise nothing
about income. Nothing here posts or sends anything; every experiment stays in `scratch/` or
drafts to a file. Setup replaces the "Good first experiments" list in `CONTEXT.md` with ideas
built on the interview; these are the generic ones.

- **[work] Time-sink audit.** Take the one task from `ABOUT-ME.md` that eats the most time. Do
  it once with Claude, timed, and write down which steps Claude did well, which it couldn't,
  and what that saves per week (honest number).
- **[work] Draft in your voice.** Paste three things you wrote (captions, messages, notes).
  Claude writes one new one in the same voice. You judge: would you send it? What gave it away?
- **[work] The spreadsheet check.** Point Claude at a CSV or spreadsheet you keep. It lists
  duplicates, blanks, and rows with no date. Read-only.
- **[hub] Handoff round trip.** With a teammate who agrees to it: hand them a small `.md` file
  through the Hub, have their Claude hand it back with a note. Time it against doing the same
  in Discord.
- **[hub] What does the Hub know about me?** `hub_whoami`, `hub_spaces_list`, `hub_briefing`.
  Read-only. Sum up what's there and what a founder can see.
- **[build] A tiny tool for something that annoys you**, built end-to-end by
  `../05-field-guide/BUILDING.md` in an afternoon.
- **[build] Rewind on purpose.** Make a mistake in a scratch file, then rewind it. Then make
  one with a command and see that rewind doesn't undo it.
- **[fun] Game helper.** Claude researches a game's current patch with `/research` and answers
  a build or strategy question, with sources and the patch date.
- **[fun] "What can you do that I don't know about?"** Claude lists its tools and connections
  right now and picks three you haven't used.

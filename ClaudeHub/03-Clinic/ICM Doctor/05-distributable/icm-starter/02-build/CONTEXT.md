# 02 — Build  ·  CONTEXT

> **Stage 2 of 3.** The workbench. Where a scoped idea becomes the real thing.

## Identity
The making room. You arrive with a brief from `01` and do the actual work — code, copy, design,
data, whatever this structure produces. Work in progress lives here until it's ready to ship.

## Inputs
- A scoped brief promoted from `01-ideate`.
- Anything the work needs from a reference shelf (add one if this structure grows).

## Process
1. **Set up** the work from the brief.
2. **Make it** — the core transformation this structure exists to do.
3. **Self-check** against the brief's "done" before handing off.

## Outputs / Handoff
- A finished piece of work.
- **Promote → `../03-ship/`** once it meets the brief.

## Conventions
- Keep one unit of work per folder/file. Don't ship from here — `03` is the dock.

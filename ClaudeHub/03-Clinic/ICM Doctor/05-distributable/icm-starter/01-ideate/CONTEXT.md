# 01 — Ideate  ·  CONTEXT

> **Stage 1 of 3.** The front door. Where a raw idea or request becomes something scoped enough to build.

## Identity
The thinking room. You come here before building, to decide what you actually want and why.
Cheap to explore, cheap to drop — most ideas should die or merge here.

## Inputs
- A raw idea, request, or "I wonder…" — from anywhere.
- Any constraints you already know (deadline, scope, the decision it serves).

## Process
1. **Capture** the idea in a short `.md` file (one per idea).
2. **Sharpen** it: the core ask in one sentence, what's in/out of scope, what "done" looks like.
3. **Triage:** drop, park, or **promote** to build.

## Outputs / Handoff
- A scoped idea ready to make.
- **Promote → `../02-build/`** — copy the scoped ask in as the starting brief.

## Conventions
- One file per idea. Messy is allowed here. Don't build yet — if you're making the thing, go to `02`.

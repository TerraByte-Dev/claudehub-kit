# 03 — Ship  ·  CONTEXT

> **Stage 3 of 3.** The loading dock. Where finished work leaves the structure — and the record
> of what shipped lives. This room is the source of truth for "what's done."

## Identity
The dock and the archive. Finished work lands here, gets sent to its destination, and stays as the
authoritative record of what was delivered. If anything disagrees about what shipped, this wins.

## Inputs
- Finished work promoted from `02-build`.

## Process
1. **Receive** the finished piece.
2. **Ship** it to its destination (publish, deliver, deploy — whatever "out" means here).
3. **Record** what shipped and when, so this room stays a trustworthy log.

## Outputs / Handoff
- Delivered work + an honest record of it. This is the terminal room.

## Conventions
- Nothing half-finished lands here. Superseded items move to `_Archive/`, never deleted.

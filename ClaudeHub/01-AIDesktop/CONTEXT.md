# 01 — AIDesktop  ·  CONTEXT

> **The desk.** Where the actual work lives. Everything else in ClaudeHub supports this folder.

## Identity
The person's projects, one folder each:

| Folder | Holds |
|--------|-------|
| `01-Personal/` | their own projects: side projects, games, hobbies, life admin. Empty until they start one |
| `02-Work/` | anything they do for money or for the crew: their desks (built from the interview, named for what they do) and `hub/`, the TerraByte Hub room |
| `03-Research/` | deep-research briefs, one folder per question, for work or personal questions alike |
| `04-Toolshed/` | free tools from GitHub, checked before they run, one folder and card each |
| `ABOUT-ME.md` | who they are, what they run, what they want. **Source of truth about the person**; written by the first-launch interview |

## Process
1. **New project** → make a folder for it in the right half, named for what it is
   (`price-tracker`, `discord-bot`). Give it a short `CONTEXT.md`: what it is, what done
   looks like, where its source of truth is.
2. **Bigger project** (several stages, will run for weeks) → lay it out with the ICM Doctor
   (`../03-Clinic/ICM Doctor/`) instead of improvising the folders.
3. **A question that needs real research** → `03-Research/`, even when it serves a project.
   The project links to the brief; the brief isn't copied into it.
4. **Working on one** → open that project's folder, so only its context loads.

## Conventions
- One project, one folder. Don't mix two projects in one folder.
- Unsure whether it's Personal or Work? Ask once, then record the answer in the project's
  `CONTEXT.md`.
- **Work stays work.** Nothing confidential about TerraByte, the crew, or anyone's clients goes
  into `01-Personal/` or `03-Research/`; personal projects stay out of `02-Work/`.
